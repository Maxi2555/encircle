Encircle makes your whole screen selectable. Call it up and the screen freezes: tap, swipe or circle what you need, then copy, share or open it. No search, no account, no internet.

SMART SELECTION
• Tap for a word, tap again for the sentence, once more for the whole paragraph
• Swipe over text like a highlighter, or diagonally across a paragraph
• Circle anything, including parts of images to share
• Handles for fine-tuning
• Copied text is reflowed into clean paragraphs, without stray hyphens

LINKS, ADDRESSES AND NUMBERS
Links, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and IBANs are recognized and underlined, even when they wrap over two lines or are written as "name (at) example (dot) org". One tap selects all of it and offers the right action: open the link, write an e-mail, call, copy the IBAN.

EVERY COMMON CODE
QR, Micro QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN, UPC, Code 128 and more. Wi-Fi codes connect you right away, contact cards go to your contacts, events to your calendar.

BEAUTIFUL AND FAST
Material You in your wallpaper's colours, light and dark, with lively animations. The frozen screen appears instantly; text and codes are recognized in parallel.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. Encircle has no internet permission and cannot send anything. No ads, no tracking, no account. Encircle is open source (Apache 2.0): github.com/Maxi2555/encircle

SETTING IT UP
Android only starts apps like this as the digital assistant. Open Encircle after installing and tap "Assistent auswählen" (choose assistant). Then start Encircle by long-pressing home or with your device's assistant gesture. The app walks you through what applies to your phone.

Notes: The user interface is in German for now. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit, running offline on the device. Apps that block screenshots (such as banking apps) cannot be read.
